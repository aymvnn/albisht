# ALBISHT — Design Context

## Users

**Primary buyer:** The father of the groom in a Qatari family of means (age 45–70). Often delegates execution to his trusted middle man (oudste zoon, oom, of familie-adviseur). They speak Arabic at home and conduct business in English.

**Context of use:** Browsing on an iPad or large monitor, late evening, in private. They are evaluating ALBISHT against memory — "deze site moet voelen zoals de Diwan eruitziet."

**Job to be done:** Vergewis je dat ALBISHT op paleisniveau werkt zonder het ook maar te hoeven uitleggen. De site is een visitekaartje vóór het eerste gesprek; verkoopt niet maar bevestigt.

**Emotional goals:** Eerbied, rust, vertrouwen. Een gevoel dat de uitvoerder de cultuur dieper kent dan jij. Geen excitement, geen enthousiasme — sereniteit en soevereiniteit.

## Brand Personality

**Three words:** *Ceremonieel · Soeverein · Ingehouden*

**Voice:** Arabisch primair (RTL), Engels secundair. Ingehouden, eerbiedig, sober. Never effusive. Never "we are passionate about creating dream weddings." Always: *"Wij voeren driehonderd ceremonies per jaar uit, elk onder hetzelfde protocol. De waardigheid komt uit de discipline, niet uit schaarste."*

**Reference points (positief):**
- Bottega Veneta (restraint as luxury signal)
- The Diwan Amiri Qatar
- New Yorker / The Gentlewoman (editorial typography)
- Aman Resorts (silence as service)

**Anti-references:**
- Generic GCC wedding planner sites (rose-gold script, bridal-bouquet hero, slow piano)
- Westerse "elegance" tropes (cursive scripts, lavender, blush pink)
- Tech-startup aesthetics (cards, gradient text, glassmorphism)

## Aesthetic Direction

**Anchor:** The Zajel brand identity book — pure black, primary gold #D28E29,
white paper, and the silk-ribbon motif. Royal-protocol restraint without
the fabricated "Pearl Court" vocabulary the older draft of this file
invented.

**Conceptual contrast:** Light paper is the home; black is the ceremonial
register; gold is the seal. The bisht reveal opens dark and resolves
into light — the only place that contrast is allowed to play out
dramatically. Elsewhere the surface stays light.

**Theme:** LIGHT-dominant. ~80% pure white #FFFFFF, with optional subtle
warm-paper for editorial long-reads (#F8F5EF). Dark surfaces appear ONLY
at the BishtReveal intro, the SiteFooter, and the /consult brief paper —
never as a section-alternation rhythm.

**Photography rule:** Frontale close-up portraits of private guests zijn
verboden om privacy-redenen. Wide ceremony scenes met zichtbare deelnemers
zijn echter geoorloofd — de Zajel brand book toont expliciet ardha sword-
dance scènes (page 20) en woestijn-/cultuur-context (page 22) met mensen
in beeld. De eerdere "geen gezichten ooit"-regel was te strikt.

**Forbidden:** Rose-gold, blush, "champagne," cursive scripts, gradient text
(buiten de officiële brand-gradient), glassmorphism, neon, dark-mode-with-
glow-accents, generic shadow-on-card, sparklines as decoration, bridal-
dress imagery, close-up portraits van privé-gasten, en de fictieve "Pearl
Court" / camelhair-weave / marble-veining motieven uit het oude plan.

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint is the luxury signal.** Less type, less color, less motion. One signature moment, not five.
2. **Two product lines, not one.** Het oude "alleen mannenzaal" was strategisch fout. ALBISHT voert zowel volledige mannen-ceremonies (vijf prijspakketten) als vrouwen-hospitality (capacity-tier service) uit. Iedere pagina respecteert beide.
3. **Arabic-first, RTL-true.** Niet een Engels-site met Arabische sticker. AR is de bron, EN is de spiegel. Numerieken in passende systeem per taal.
4. **Anonymity in marketing.** Geen familienamen, geen identificeerbare close-ups van privé-gasten. Wel: ceremoniescenes en cultuurcontext mogen mensen in beeld hebben — privacy ligt in de framing, niet in een totaalverbod.
5. **Het ritueel is de interface.** /consult is geen formulier, het is een brief. De stuur-knop is een lakzegel. De microcopy spreekt zoals een protocol-officier zou spreken.
6. **Single source of truth.** Bij twijfel over kleur, font, logo of motief: open [Identity_al bisht_2.pdf](../Identity_al bisht_2.pdf) of [albisht-brand/tokens.json](../albisht-brand/tokens.json). Niet improviseren.

## Type System (locked in)

The original spec for this file (Cinzel/Italiana/Cardo for Latin) was an
**invention** that predates the Zajel brand identity book. The book
prescribes **Adobe Arabic** as a single family for both scripts. The site
uses the closest free Google-Fonts analogues:

**Latin display + body:** **EB Garamond** (400 / 500 / 600 / 700)
**Arabic display:** **Amiri 700** (clean classical Naskh designed for display)
**Arabic body:** **Markazi Text** (400 / 500 / 600 / 700)
**Calligraphic mark:** The ALBISHT calligraphic mark is an SVG, not a font.
It's the brand's only "calligraphic" element. Used as a stand-alone visual
mark for **large titles only** (max 5 placements site-wide). Do NOT use it
as a body or headline typeface.

> DecoType Thuluth was previously chained in as the Arabic display fallback.
> Removed — its thin strokes and floating tashkeel marks fragmented on
> screen at display sizes.

## Color System

> ⚠️ The previous "Pearl Court" palette (`#C9A24B` zari-gold, `#F6F1E6` pearl,
> `#EFE7D4` ivory, `#0B0B0A` ink-bisht, `#3F4A33` olive, etc.) was a fiction
> not present in the brand book. Treat any reference to those token names
> in older code as legacy. The real palette is below; canonical machine-
> readable source is [albisht-brand/tokens.json](../albisht-brand/tokens.json).

The official ALBISHT palette per the Zajel brand book:

| Token (preferred) | Hex      | Role |
|-------------------|----------|------|
| `--color-zari`    | `#D28E29` | Primary gold. The brand's defining colour. ~8% of pixel weight. |
| `--color-zari-mid`   | `#E29F29` | Gradient bottom stop. |
| `--color-zari-bright`| `#F6B62B` | Gradient top stop. |
| `--color-ink`     | `#000000` | Pure brand black. Used for headlines on light and as the dark accent surface (BishtReveal, footer, /consult brief). |
| `--color-pearl`   | `#FFFFFF` | Pure white. The primary surface for ~80% of all pages. |
| `--color-ink-soft`| `#1A1A1A` | Body text on light surfaces (support, not brand-defined). |
| (optional) paper-warm | `#F8F5EF` | Subtle warm-paper variant for editorial long-reads. |

**Surface usage rule:** Light is the default surface. Dark surfaces are
reserved for ceremonially significant moments — the BishtReveal intro,
the SiteFooter, and the /consult brief paper. **Do NOT alternate dark/light
between sections** — the alternation strobes the eye.

**Gold gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #E29F29 0%, #F6B62B 100%)` —
reserved for the calligraphic mark gradient variant, the silk-ribbon motif,
and the Top-VIP premium tier accent.
